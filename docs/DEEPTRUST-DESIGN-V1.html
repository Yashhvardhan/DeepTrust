01

No such circular exists. The document is a re-typed 2016 press note. The reference number on it (DCM/1284/2026) does not resolve in the central bank's public circular index, and the seal is a raster copy lifted from a 2019 PDF.

02

The photo was altered in three places. The banner text was replaced, currency bundles were inserted onto the podium, and the press row was cloned to look twice as crowded. The original frame is in a wire archive dated 8 August.

03

The video is real but unrelated. It is 41 seconds from a routine policy briefing on monetary transmission, cut at 0:18 so the sentence “no change to the currency in circulation” never plays.

04

Everything traces to one anonymous forward. All 38 reposts descend from a single Telegram message at 04:12 IST. Zero tier-1 outlets carry the claim; two have published explicit denials.
